Phone app

Record a ride

Point the phone's back camera at the road and drive. When you press Stop, the video and GPS track are uploaded and every pothole and crack is pinned on the map.

Camera and GPS are blocked on this connection. Browsers only allow them on HTTPS or localhost. On a phone over Wi-Fi, open chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure, add and relaunch Chrome, or use an HTTPS tunnel (see README). You can still upload a photo or video from the camera app (step 3).
