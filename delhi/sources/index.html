Sources & evidence

Where all of this comes from.

Nagrik does not produce data. It collects the records Delhi’s civic bodies already publish — tenders, ward boundaries, officers’ directories — and puts them on one map. Everything below is a public record. This page says exactly which one, and what to do when we get something wrong.

This is not an official government site.
Nagrik is an independent civic-data project, not affiliated with or operated by any municipal corporation or government body. Records here are copies, and copies go stale. Before you rely on anything — a tender value, a deadline, an officer’s phone number — verify it with the body directly. Every record links back to its official source for exactly that reason.

What we take, and from where

DataSource

  • MCD’s 250 ward names and boundaries

    Ward geometry from the Esri Living Atlas India ward layer, joined to MCD's own published ward names.

    livingatlas.esri.in

  • MCD’s 12 zone boundaries

    Zone geometry from the Esri Living Atlas India town-zone layer. NDMC and the Delhi Cantonment appear as single, un-subdivided areas.

    livingatlas.esri.in

  • MCD councillors, officers and helplines

    The Municipal Corporation of Delhi's own portal. elected members, zonal officers and switchboard lines.

    mcdonline.nic.in

  • Tenders and awarded contracts

    Delhi's NIC eProcurement portal, covering MCD and the other executing bodies that tender through it.

    govtprocurement.delhi.gov.in

  • Place search (localities and landmarks)

    Ward names resolve against MCD's own boundaries offline; an OpenStreetMap extract adds colonies and landmarks by name.

    OpenStreetMap

  • Base map tiles

    Vector tiles served free and keyless by OpenFreeMap. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the ODbL.

    OpenFreeMap · OpenStreetMap
How current it is
  • Tenders and officials refresh nightly. Boundaries are updated when redrawn.
  • Awards, cancellations and retenders follow the published portal results.
  • Tenders removed from the portal are marked closed and retained.
What we will not do

We never invent a record to fill a gap. Where an authority does not publish something — a councillor’s phone number, a ward’s zone — Nagrik shows nothing rather than a plausible guess. A blank means the government has not published it, not that we forgot to look.

Officials’ contact details

Nagrik republishes the names, designations, office phone numbers and official email addresses that these bodies already publish in their own public directories. These are official points of contact for public duties, reproduced so a resident can reach the person accountable for their ward. No personal or private contact information is collected, inferred, or shown. If you are named here and want your entry corrected or removed, write to us.

Get something corrected

Wrong number, wrong ward, wrong name, a work attributed to the wrong agency — tell us and we will fix it, or explain why the record says what it says.
