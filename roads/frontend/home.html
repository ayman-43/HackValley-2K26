01 / Detect

One pothole, counted once.

A video sees the same pothole in dozens of frames. Nagrik Roads tracks each one across frames and pins it once, at the GPS point where it was seen. Drive the same road again and it updates the old record instead of adding a new one.

Why Nagrik Roads

See the damage. Count it once. Fix the worst first. Prove it was fixed.

Potholes get reported over and over, then forgotten. Nagrik Roads turns a phone camera into a road survey: every defect gets one record, a location, a risk score and a history, so the city can see what is broken and whether it was repaired.

Roads are public. Their condition should be too.

Nagrik Roads
Hackathon prototype

Nagrik Roads is a student project built for HackValley 2026. It is not an official BBMP or GBA system. Ward boundaries are from Nagrik; detections are made by an AI model and can be wrong.
