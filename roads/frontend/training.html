How training works

1. Collect

Four public road-damage datasets from India and the US, all labelled with boxes around each defect.

2. Unify

Every dataset is converted to the same YOLO format and the same four classes, then split 83% train and 17% validation with a fixed seed.

3. Train

YOLO26n on the laptop and the larger YOLO26s on a Kaggle GPU, starting from weights pre-trained on COCO.

4. Pick the winner

Both are scored on the Indian validation images. The best one replaces the current model in the app.
